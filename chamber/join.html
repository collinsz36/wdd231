NP Membership

This membership is designed for non-profit organizations and has no membership fee.

  • Free membership
  • Business directory listing
  • Networking opportunities
  • Access to selected chamber events

Bronze Membership

Bronze membership provides affordable benefits for growing businesses.

  • Business directory listing
  • Networking opportunities
  • Access to chamber events
  • Selected event discounts

Silver Membership

Silver membership provides additional promotional and training opportunities.

  • All Bronze membership benefits
  • Business training opportunities
  • Advertising opportunities
  • Event discounts
  • Special member events

Gold Membership

Gold membership provides premium promotional opportunities for businesses.

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Home page spotlight opportunities
  • Premium business promotion
  • Special networking events
  • Premium training opportunities
